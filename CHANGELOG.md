# Changelog

Changes to the library, guidance and tools. Your own program's history lives in `program/records/audit-trail.csv` and git.

## 1.0.0 – 2026-11-23

First public release, from the Compliance Brain template of 16 November 2026.

- 185 controls with scoping rules, 15 policy blueprints with 144 statements, 22-document detailed policy library, 35 risks, vendor tiers and categories, 35 CUECs, settings to confirm, requirement lines and privacy obligations.
- AI Guidance sections 0 to 10, including control framework first, resolving detail when it matters, and sizing the program to the company.
- Company profile with 93 fields and their options.
- Program skeleton: context and context sources, control list, 15 policies, registers, records with an audit trail for people and AI, 7 record templates and 4 plans.
- `tools/scope.py` (matches the Google Sheets scoping) and `tools/check.py`.
