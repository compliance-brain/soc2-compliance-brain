# Evidence guide

> [!NOTE]
> **🧭 Guide.** Keep only what proves a control worked. Never alter, back-date or manufacture evidence, and have the Compliance Owner check anything before it reaches an auditor (AI Guidance 0.7 and 4.4).

## Where evidence lives

- **Records here:** policies, registers, sign-offs, acknowledgements, the activity log, tickets and people. Git history shows who changed what and when.
- **Files:** screenshots, exports and reports in [program/evidence/](../evidence/) or `{{evidence folder link}}`, with one folder per audit period and one subfolder per control ID, for example `2026-Q1/AC-03A/`.

## Capturing evidence

- Show the system, the page and the date.
- One screenshot of a setting at a point in the period is normally enough (AI Guidance 4.6).
- Never capture passwords, keys, customer content or health information.
- Link the folder from the control's row in the control list or the activity log row.

## Type 1 and Type 2

- **Type 1** checks controls are in place on one date, so capture configuration evidence close to it. The product must be live by then.
- **Type 2** checks controls operated over a period (often 3 to 12 months), so recurring records must cover the whole period.
