## Purpose

Sets out how personal information is collected, used, shared, kept and protected, and how individuals' rights are handled.

## Scope

Personal information the company collects or processes about customers, their users and staff.

## Statements and when they apply

<details>
<summary>8 more library statements, and when each applies</summary>

- **Privacy notice** (POL-14.01; controls PR-01). *Applies when:* All stages, when Privacy is in scope.  
  A privacy notice explains what personal information is collected, why, how it is used and shared, and individuals' rights.
- **Consent and choice** (POL-14.02; controls PR-02). *Applies when:* All stages, when Privacy is in scope.  
  Where consent is needed, it is obtained and recorded, and individuals can withdraw it.
- **Collection** (POL-14.03; controls PR-03). *Applies when:* All stages, when Privacy is in scope.  
  Only the personal information needed for the stated purposes is collected.
- **Use and retention** (POL-14.04; controls PR-04). *Applies when:* All stages, when Privacy is in scope.  
  Personal information is used only for its stated purposes and deleted when no longer needed.
- **Individual rights** (POL-14.05; controls PR-05). *Applies when:* All stages, when Privacy is in scope.  
  Requests to access, correct or delete personal information are verified and answered within legal timeframes.
- **Disclosure to third parties** (POL-14.06; controls PR-06). *Applies when:* All stages, when Privacy is in scope.  
  Personal information is shared with third parties only as described in the privacy notice and under written agreements.
- **Privacy breaches** (POL-14.07; controls PR-07). *Applies when:* All stages, when Privacy is in scope.  
  Privacy breaches are assessed and notified to individuals and regulators as the law in each region requires.
- **Enquiries and complaints** (POL-14.08; controls PR-08). *Applies when:* All stages, when Privacy is in scope.  
  Privacy enquiries and complaints are recorded and answered by {{privacy contact: TBC}}.
