## Purpose

Sets out how personal information is collected, used, shared, kept and protected, and how individuals' rights are handled.

## Scope

Personal information the company collects or processes about customers, their users and staff.

## Policy statements

### Privacy notice

A privacy notice explains what personal information is collected, why, how it is used and shared, and individuals' rights.

<sub>POL-14.01 · Controls: PR-01 · Tailor using: Privacy Policy (link)</sub>

### Consent and choice

Where consent is needed, it is obtained and recorded, and individuals can withdraw it.

<sub>POL-14.02 · Controls: PR-02 · Tailor using: Regulatory Drivers</sub>

### Collection

Only the personal information needed for the stated purposes is collected.

<sub>POL-14.03 · Controls: PR-03 · Tailor using: Data minimisation</sub>

### Use and retention

Personal information is used only for its stated purposes and deleted when no longer needed.

<sub>POL-14.04 · Controls: PR-04 · Tailor using: Data Handled</sub>

### Individual rights

Requests to access, correct or delete personal information are verified and answered within legal timeframes.

<sub>POL-14.05 · Controls: PR-05 · Tailor using: Regulatory Drivers, Customer Geography</sub>

### Disclosure to third parties

Personal information is shared with third parties only as described in the privacy notice and under written agreements.

<sub>POL-14.06 · Controls: PR-06 · Tailor using: Subservice Providers, Vendors tab</sub>

### Privacy breaches

Privacy breaches are assessed and notified to individuals and regulators as the law in each region requires.

<sub>POL-14.07 · Controls: PR-07 · Tailor using: Country/Region(s), Customer Geography</sub>

### Enquiries and complaints

Privacy enquiries and complaints are recorded and answered by {{privacy contact}}.

<sub>POL-14.08 · Controls: PR-08 · Tailor using: Team Details</sub>
