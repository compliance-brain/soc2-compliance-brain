## Purpose

Sets out how staff may use company systems, devices, data and AI tools.

## Scope

All staff and contractors with access to company systems or data.

## Policy statements

### Acknowledgement

Staff read and accept this policy and the information security policies when they join and after material changes.

<sub>POL-02.01 · Controls: GV-06 · Tailor using: HR System, Knowledge Management</sub>

### Use of company systems

Company systems and data are used for business purposes. Staff don't share accounts, turn off security tools or keep company data in unapproved services.

<sub>POL-02.02 · Controls: GV-05 · Tailor using: Workspace, Data Handled</sub>

### Credentials

Staff keep credentials private, store them in {{password manager: 1Password}} and turn on multi-factor authentication wherever it's offered.

<sub>POL-02.03 · Controls: GV-05 · Tailor using: Password Manager, Identity and Authentication</sub>

### Devices

Work is done on devices that meet company security settings: disk encryption, screen lock, automatic updates and malware protection.

<sub>POL-02.04 · Controls: GV-05 · Tailor using: Device Management, Endpoint model</sub>

### AI tools

Staff use only approved AI tools for company work, and enter customer or confidential data only into tools approved for that data.

<sub>POL-02.06 · Controls: AI-02 · Tailor using: AI Models and Platforms, Data Handled</sub>

### Reporting

Staff report lost devices, suspected phishing and other security concerns straight away through {{reporting channel: security@example.com}}.

<sub>POL-02.07 · Controls: GV-05 · Tailor using: Incident response</sub>
