## Purpose

Sets out how the company identifies, rates, treats and monitors risks to its service and commitments, including fraud.

## Scope

Risks to the in-scope system, the company and its customers.

## Policy statements

### Risk framework

Risks are recorded in the risk register with an owner, a likelihood and impact rating and a treatment decision, using the method on the Risks tab.

<sub>POL-06.01 · Controls: RM-01 · Tailor using: Risks tab</sub>

### Risk assessment (periodic)

A risk assessment is completed at least annually and when major changes happen. It considers fraud, vendors, AI and changes to the business.

<sub>POL-06.02 · Controls: RM-02A · Tailor using: Risk management, Planned Changes</sub>

### Risk assessment (continuous)

Risks are identified and updated as part of normal operations, and the whole register is reviewed at least annually.

<sub>POL-06.03 · Controls: RM-02B · Tailor using: Risk management</sub>

### Risk treatment

Each risk rated above the company's risk appetite has a treatment plan. Management formally accepts any risk that remains.

<sub>POL-06.04 · Controls: RM-03 · Tailor using: Risks tab: Risk appetite</sub>

### Risk appetite

Management sets and records the level of risk it is prepared to accept, and reviews it annually.

<sub>POL-06.05 · Controls: RM-05 · Tailor using: Compliance Goals, Customer Types</sub>

### Fraud

Each risk assessment considers how fraud could happen, including management overriding controls, and the controls that prevent it.

<sub>POL-06.06 · Controls: RM-01 · Tailor using: Headcount, Governance</sub>
