## Purpose

Sets the behaviour expected of staff, including integrity, confidentiality and reporting misconduct, and the consequences of breaches.

## Scope

All staff and contractors.

## Policy statements

### Standards

Staff act with integrity, treat others with respect, avoid conflicts of interest and protect confidential information.

<sub>POL-03.01 · Controls: GV-04 · Tailor using: Existing handbook or code of conduct</sub>

### Acknowledgement

Staff acknowledge the code of conduct when they join.

<sub>POL-03.02 · Controls: GV-04 · Tailor using: HR System</sub>

### Fraud and misconduct

Fraud, theft and misuse of company or customer assets are prohibited. Staff can report suspected misconduct without fear of retaliation.

<sub>POL-03.03 · Controls: GV-04 · Tailor using: Governance Lead</sub>

### Disciplinary process

Breaches of this code or the security policies are handled through a disciplinary process proportionate to the breach.
