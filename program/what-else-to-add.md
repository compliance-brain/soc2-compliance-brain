## Context that changes obligations rather than controls

| If… | Then |
| --- | --- |
| You handle health information | Ask whether you are a business associate or covered entity (AI Guidance 9.3). Add the HIPAA requirement line, business associate agreements on the vendor register, and HIPAA breach notice timeframes to the incident plan (10.2). |
| You handle payment card data | Ask whether card numbers touch your systems. If they do, PCI DSS applies; if only your payment provider's, record that in the system description. |
| Children can use the service | Add consent, privacy and safety obligations for the countries you serve. |
| You sell to consumers as well as businesses | Settle where you are controller and where processor, then add privacy statements and rights-request handling. |
| You operate or store data in more countries | Add the privacy laws for each (requirement lines and privacy obligations in the library) and data-location statements. |
| Customers need a specific report or framework | Add the framework to Framework Scope and build a crosswalk (AI Guidance 7.5). |

Policies and statements follow the controls: a statement comes into scope when one of its linked controls does. Each policy lists its other statements, and when they apply, under **Add if your setup differs**.
