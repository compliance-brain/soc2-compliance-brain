## Outline (about 20 minutes)

1. **Why it matters here:** what we hold (customer content and personal data) and what customers expect of us.
2. **Phishing and social engineering:** how to spot it, and reporting it in `{{incident channel: #incidents in Slack}}`. Never approve unexpected MFA prompts.
3. **Accounts:** our single sign-on through `{{identity provider: Google Workspace}}`, MFA everywhere, and passwords only in `{{password manager: 1Password}}`.
4. **Devices:** managed by `{{device management: Kandji}}`, kept updated and locked. Report a lost device straight away.
5. **Data handling:** our four classes (Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted); customer data stays in approved systems.
6. **AI tools:** only approved tools, and no customer or confidential data unless the tool is approved for it (AI Acceptable Use).
7. **Reporting incidents and concerns:** what counts, who to tell, and that reporting quickly is always right.
8. **Policies:** where to find them, and acknowledging them.
