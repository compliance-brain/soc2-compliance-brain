## Scenario (pick one, or write your own)

- A leaked production secret is found in a public repository.
- A customer reports seeing another customer's data.
- An engineer's laptop is stolen while signed in.
- `{{cloud provider: Amazon Web Services}}` has a regional outage during business hours.

## Walk-through prompts

1. Who notices first, and how is it reported?
2. Who leads, and how is severity set?
3. What do we contain first? Who can do it, and with what access?
4. What evidence do we keep?
5. Who must be told (customers, regulators, insurers), and by when?
6. How do we recover and confirm it's fixed?
