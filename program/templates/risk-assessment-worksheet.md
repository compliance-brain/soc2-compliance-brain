# Risk assessment worksheet

> [!NOTE]
> **🧭 Guide.** Controls RM-01, RM-02A and RM-03. Once a year, and when something significant changes. Work through the [risk register](../registers/risk-register.csv) in one sitting (an hour for a team of 10), using the scales in the [library method](../../brain/library/README.md#risk-rating). Record the review in the [sign-off log](../records/sign-off-log.csv) as a Risk register review.

**Date:** `{{date}}`
**Attendees:** `{{CEO, CTO, Head of Operations}}`

1. **What changed since last time?** New products, customers, regions, vendors, people, incidents, audit findings. Check [what-else-to-add.md](../what-else-to-add.md).
2. **New risks?** Look at the library's 35 risks ([risks.csv](../../brain/library/risks.csv)) for any that now apply.
3. **Re-rate each risk:** likelihood and impact from 1 to 5. Do the linked controls still operate?
4. **Above appetite?** Anything rated `{{risk appetite: High (10) or above}}` needs a treatment plan with an owner and date, or a recorded acceptance.
5. **Fraud:** consider how someone could misuse access or divert money (RM-03, POL-06.06).
