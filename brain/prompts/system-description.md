# System description prompts

Prompts for drafting the five narrative fields of the SOC 2 system description in [program/context/system-description.md](../../program/context/system-description.md). Give your agent the company profile with each prompt. Replace `{{Company Name}}` and `{{Domain}}` first.

## Company Description

You are drafting a first-cut SOC 2 system description for {{Company Name}} (client website: {{Domain}}). Treat the Company Context, System Context and Compliance Shapes answers in the company profile as facts provided by the client. Search the web to research the client website, its published terms of service, privacy policy, SLA, DPA, security or trust pages, and reputable public sources. Stick to facts about what exists today; do not guess or repeat marketing claims. After each fact add its source in brackets, e.g. [Source: URL] or [Source: company profile]. Where a fact needs a document that is not public, insert a bracketed placeholder such as [REQUIRES MSA TEMPLATE TO COMPLETE], [REQUIRES SLA TO COMPLETE] or [REQUIRES CLIENT CONFIRMATION]. Return only the field text, with no heading. Draft the Company Description field in 2-4 sentences: legal and trading name, year founded, headquarters, what the company does, and the industries and customer types it serves.
